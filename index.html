How it works

One mailing a month, on a fixed schedule.

Each month follows the same four steps, so you always know the next deadline and what’s needed from you.

  1. By the 15th

    Book your spot

    Choose a spot size and your area, and reserve space on next month’s postcard.

    You: package, area, logo, offer and any photos.

  2. By the 20th

    Approve your design

    We lay out your spot and send you a proof. Nothing goes to print until you approve it.

    Spotix designs, you approve.

  3. By the 1st

    Printed and mailed

    The postcard is printed and sent out through Canada Post to 10,000 homes.

    Spotix: printing and distribution.

  4. After delivery

    Count the responses

    QR scans and calls to your tracking number show what the card brought in. Use them to shape next month’s offer.

    You: watch responses, adjust your next offer.

Dates are the targets for each monthly cycle. Delivery is handled by Canada Post and its timing isn’t guaranteed — see section 9 of our Terms of Service.

Measuring results

See what the card brings in.

A postcard is only worth repeating if you can tell whether it worked. Larger spots have tracking built into the ad.

  • QR code

    Duo Spot and Full Spot

    Printed on your spot and pointed at your website or booking page, so scans from the card can be counted.

  • Tracking phone number

    Full Spot

    A dedicated number on your spot, so calls from the postcard are kept apart from your other calls.

  • Your own offer code

    Any spot

    Ask customers to mention or enter a code from the card. It’s the simplest way to count responses on a Solo Spot.

Tracking only counts people who use the code or number. Some will simply walk in, so treat the counts as a minimum, not the full picture.

A postcard and online ads work differently
AspectNeighbourhood postcardOnline ads
Where it appearsA printed card delivered to the homeA placement on a screen, in a feed or search results
How it’s aimedBy delivery areaBy platform audience settings and keywords
How cost worksA fixed price per spot, known upfrontOften auction-based, so cost varies with competition
How long it’s aroundUntil the household recycles itWhile it’s on screen
How you measure itQR codes, tracking numbers, offer codesPlatform reports on impressions and clicks

Neither is right for every business, and many local businesses use both: a postcard reaches homes in a defined area, while online ads reach people who are already searching.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What exactly is Spotix?

A shared direct-mail postcard for Toronto businesses. You book a spot on the card; we design your ad, print the postcard and send it through Canada Post to 10,000 homes.

Which neighbourhoods can I target?

Targeting is by Toronto postal-code area, grouped by neighbourhood. The areas we currently list are shown under Coverage. If yours isn’t there, ask and we’ll confirm whether it can be included.

What if I don’t have a design?

Design is included with every spot. Send your logo and offer and we’ll handle the layout. You get a proof to approve before anything goes to print, so no design skills are needed.

How do I track results?

Duo Spot includes a QR code. Full Spot adds a dedicated tracking phone number as well. On a Solo Spot, a unique offer code that customers mention or enter works well. See Measuring results.

Can I advertise more than once?

Yes. Monthly subscriptions are available with a 15% discount, and you can change your offer from one month to the next.

When do I pay?

Full payment is required before production begins. We accept major credit cards and electronic transfers, and all prices are in Canadian dollars.

Can I cancel?

Cancellations made at least 14 days before the scheduled mailing date are eligible for a full refund. Refunds aren’t available once production has begun. The details are in our Terms of Service.

Is delivery guaranteed?

No. Our mailing dates are targets, and delivery is carried out by Canada Post. As our Terms explain, delivery dates are estimates and can be affected by weather, postal service issues or other factors outside our control.

Who else is on the card?

Other local businesses that have booked spots on the same mailing. All ads have to follow our content guidelines, which are set out in section 6 of the Terms of Service.
